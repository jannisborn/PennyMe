# PennyMe Terms of Use & Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 22, 2026

By using PennyMe, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, you may not use the app.

## 1. Community contributions

PennyMe lets people anonymously suggest machine information and share comments and images. There are no user accounts. Contributions are public and may be edited or removed.

## 2. Zero tolerance

We do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive users. Do not submit illegal, fraudulent, sexually explicit, hateful, harassing, threatening, violent, discriminatory, misleading, spam, private, or otherwise harmful content. Do not impersonate others or violate their rights.

## 3. Moderation and enforcement

Submitted text may be filtered automatically. Anyone can report objectionable content from a machine view and block its contributor. Blocking hides attributed content immediately on that device and sends a report to PennyMe. We review reports within several working days. We may remove content and restrict or permanently eject the contributor, including through anonymous installation or network identifiers.

## 4. Your content

Only submit content you have the right to share. You grant PennyMe permission to store, process, adapt, display, and distribute your contribution for operating and improving the service.

## 5. Account-free identifiers

PennyMe does not create user accounts. For moderation and abuse prevention, the app creates a random installation identifier. Public blocking uses only a one-way pseudonym; the random identifier is not shown to other users.

## 6. Safety and accuracy

Community contributions may be inaccurate. Use appropriate care when visiting a location or following external links. PennyMe is provided as-is without a guarantee that every listing or contribution is complete, current, or available.

## 7. Termination

We may remove content or restrict access without notice when these Terms are violated or community safety is at risk.

## 8. Contact

Report objectionable content with the flag button in any machine view. For other safety concerns, contact [jannis.born@gmx.de](mailto:jannis.born@gmx.de).

By choosing **Agree & Continue** in the app, you confirm that you have read and accept these Terms.
